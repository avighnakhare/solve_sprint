# Leaderboard imagery

## `recognition-hero.webp`

- Path: `/public/images/leaderboard/recognition-hero.webp`
- Preferred composition: wide 3:2 or 4:3
- Recommended source size: at least 1800 × 1200
- Subject: a student team presenting or discussing completed work with a reviewer,
  judge, teacher, or organization representative
- Direction: focused and proud, warm natural light, real notebooks, sketches,
  prototypes, or presentation material
- Avoid: trophies toward camera, handshakes, graduation imagery, embedded text,
  holograms, cut-off faces, and obvious AI artifacts
- Palette: warm cream, navy, amber, and restrained sky blue
- Focal point: center, slightly upper-middle

Until this file is supplied, the leaderboard uses the existing submission-review
photograph as a safe local fallback.

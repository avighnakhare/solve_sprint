# Questions for qualified legal review

This document does not make legal conclusions.

## Minors and consent

- What age thresholds and parental-consent flows apply in launch jurisdictions?
- What evidence of consent, if any, should be retained and for how long?
- May schools or organizations invite students directly, and under what data-sharing terms?

## Privacy

- Which student fields are necessary for eligibility, and can city/state/school be minimized?
- What public portfolio fields may be shown by default or only by opt-in?
- What deletion, access, correction, export, and retention rights must be supported?
- What contracts and notices are required for D1, Resend, hosting, analytics, and monitoring providers?

## Intellectual property and submissions

- Who owns submitted work and licenses needed for judging, display, and promotion?
- What separate terms are required for organization-provided confidential information?
- How should third-party code, media, and AI-assisted work be disclosed?

## Challenge operations

- What official rules, eligibility restrictions, tax disclosures, prize terms, and dispute procedures are required?
- What review and conflict-of-interest requirements should apply to judges?
- What safety restrictions are required for medical, financial, employment, research, and regulated-industry challenges?

## Communications and marketing

- Which transactional messages require consent or unsubscribe controls?
- What restrictions apply to sponsor branding and marketing to minors?

## Incident response

- What breach-notification, safeguarding, moderation, law-enforcement, and record-retention procedures are required?

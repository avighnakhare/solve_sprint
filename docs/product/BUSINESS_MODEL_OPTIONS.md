# Business model options

No pricing or payment functionality is implemented.

| Model | Customer | Value delivered | Operational burden | Scalability | Trust risk | Student-equity impact | Required capabilities |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Per-challenge hosting | Company/nonprofit/school | One structured, reviewed student sprint | High per launch if managed manually | Medium | Pressure to approve weak briefs | Low if participation stays free | Verification, review SLA, billing, reporting |
| Annual organization subscription | Repeat host | Multiple briefs, managers, judges, analytics | Medium | High | Feature pressure and sponsor influence | Low to medium | Organization roles, quotas, billing, analytics |
| School/district license | School system | Curriculum-aligned challenge participation and reporting | High onboarding/privacy burden | Medium | Student surveillance or forced participation | Medium to high | Tenant controls, consent, reporting, data agreements |
| Managed sprint service | Organization | Brief design, moderation, operations, judging support | Very high | Low | Service quality and conflict risk | Low if access remains open | Internal operations tooling, SLAs, staffing |
| Sponsored league partnership | Sponsor | Branded theme or season with public recognition | High governance burden | Medium | Sponsor influence, student data, advertising | Medium | Brand policy, public disclosure, approval governance |

## Recommended sequence

1. Validate per-challenge hosting operationally without payment.
2. Build verification, organization roles, judge management, result publication, and privacy-safe reporting.
3. Evaluate annual organization subscriptions only after repeat-host demand is proven.
4. Keep core student discovery, team tools, submission, recognition, and basic portfolio free.
